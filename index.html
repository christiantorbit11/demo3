The Atelier

Some cars are driven. Ours are restored — panel by panel, reflection by reflection, until the paint remembers what it looked like under showroom light.

Founded on a single obsession: that a vehicle's finish is not a surface, but a record — of weather, of time, of neglect. OBSIDIAN exists to reverse that record, one stage of correction at a time.

Every detail is performed by hand, in your driveway or ours, using tools and compounds reserved for the world's most exacting concours restorers. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is templated.

Services

The Full Ritual

01

Paint Correction

Multi-stage machine polishing that erases swirl marks, oxidation, and years of neglect — revealing the true depth of your paint's original color.

02

Ceramic Coating

A 9H hydrophobic shield fused directly to your clear coat — engineered for a decade of gloss retention, chemical resistance, and effortless maintenance.

03

Interior Restoration

Deep steam extraction, leather conditioning, and complete odor elimination — your cabin returned to its first day, inside and out.

04

White-Glove Mobile Service

Our atelier comes to you. A fully equipped mobile studio, generator-powered and climate-controlled, anywhere within the metro.

Process

Four Stages. No Shortcuts.

01

Consultation & Inspection

Every vehicle begins under raking light — swirls, oxidation, and prior repairs mapped by hand before a single tool touches the paint.

02

Decontamination Wash

A pH-neutral foam bath lifts grime and bonded contaminants without a single microfiber touching the surface — the last step before correction begins.

03

Correction & Refinement

Dual-action and rotary polishers, matched compound by compound to your paint's hardness, remove defects in successive, increasingly fine stages.

04

Seal & Cure

A ceramic topcoat is applied panel by panel and cured under controlled UV — locking in the correction for years, not weeks.
